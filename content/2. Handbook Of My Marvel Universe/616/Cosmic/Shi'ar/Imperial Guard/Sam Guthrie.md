# Unresolved Plots

## External
>[!aside | show-title  right] 'Nuff Said
>I dunno if Sam should be immortal, I dunno if anyone should ever even use the Externals anymore.  But ya know, that doesn't mean you leave it hanging, and maybe you can do something cool with it?

It has been claimed by some of the Externals that Cannonball is one of them, and therefore immortal, although his status as one of them has been brought into question. Cable initially believed he was an External after a future Professor search revealed that Cannonball lived well into the 24th century. Upon his awakening, from his first death, five Externals were seen sensing that Cannonball was one of them. Stryfe was positive that Cannonball was one of these immortal Externals. Selene, another External, would deny him ever having been one of them and called into question Cable's conclusion and the reasons for it. Cable would become unsure if Sam was an External or not, after all his younger brother, Icarus, had a healing factor; that could very well run in the family. Years later, when Cable was asked about Sam's status as an External, he chalked it up to a rumor.

Cannonball is a [[2. Handbook Of My Marvel Universe/616/Mutants/Mutants\|mutant]].

**Thermo-Chemical Energy Field Propulsion**: Cannonball possesses the ability to bodily generate thermo-chemical energy and release it from his skin. This energy is used as thrust to cause his body to be propelled through the air, like his namesake, at great heights and speeds with considerable maneuverability. He can control his speed and direction through sheer act of will. At first, he could only release this energy from his feet, but now he can fire it from almost any part of his body to a wide variety of effects, such as channeling it into bio-kinetic blasts from his hands. His power levels have varied over the years - but at his peak, he has been able to defeat the Shi'ar Imperial Guard leader, Gladiator and devastate several city blocks by plummeting to the ground from high altitude.

**Blast Field**: This energy also manifests itself as an impenetrable and virtually indestructible "blast field." He can use this blast-field to function as a personal shield or extending it to encompass others, shape the field around another person to imprison them, or absorb outside kinetic impact into his own energy supply and then re-channel it to increase the bludgeoning power of his blows or create explosive shock waves upon impact. Sam is also capable of generating the blast field without actually having to blast off, though it takes him extreme concentration to prevent himself from propelling into the air.

>[!aside | show-title  left] 'Nuff Said
>Did you know, [[2. Handbook Of My Marvel Universe/616/Cosmic/Shi'ar/Imperial Guard/Sam Guthrie\|Cannonball]] is nigh-invulnerable while blastin' ?

**Superhuman Durability**: When he is using his superhuman powers, Cannonball possesses superhuman durability due to the energy field that forms around his body.
